Frogger Invaders

Classic Space Invaders pressure, rethemed as a midnight frog-crossing showdown.

Move with Left / Right or A / D, fire with Space, restart with Enter.

Hero: a roadside frog launcher guarding the crossing lanes.
Invaders: neon traffic critters descend in formation and fire back.
Theme: river sky, painted lanes, headlights, and arcade HUD styling inspired by Frogger.
